Risk, book-keeping and trade operations for a book that is live on six commodity exchanges.

  • 02Book-keepingAfter each session, agents reconcile our positions against the broker's and the targets. A one-lot break is reported, not netted away.
  • 03Trade operationsAgents check every strategy and data feed after each session and file a verdict. People read the exceptions.
