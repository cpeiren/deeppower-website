  • 02Tested without hindsightWe report two numbers: what perfect price knowledge would save, and what a controller that sees only forecasts actually saves. Most claims quote only the first.

20 to 22 January 2025, simulated 500 kW site in Dallas with a 0.5 MW / 2 MWh battery. PowerNet moves flexible jobs and charges the battery when power is cheap, then discharges when it is expensive. It paid $39/MWh for its energy; the unscheduled site paid $43/MWh.

Simulated 500 kW GPU site in Dallas (ERCOT North load zone) with a 0.5 MW / 2 MWh battery, real 2025 real-time prices and temperatures, three two-week windows starting 15 January, 1 April and 1 November. Optimized schedules push some deferrable work past the end of the window, so results are per MWh of compute delivered inside it. Summer windows are left out: their savings depend on knowing ERCOT's four coincident-peak hours in advance.
