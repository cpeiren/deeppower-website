How the numbers are built

Forecast. A new run every hour, each covering the next 43 to 48 hours. The scored value is the median of the price distribution.

Same hour, last known day. The actual price of the same hour on the most recent day already settled when the forecast was made.

Trailing 7-day median. The median of the 168 hourly prices known when the forecast was made.

Spike flag. An hour is flagged when the top of its 80% range (the 90th percentile) is $100 or more.
